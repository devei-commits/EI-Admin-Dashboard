================================================================================
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

EI-Admin is a comprehensive admin panel for Esthetic Insights, a cosmetics/
pharmaceutical manufacturing company. The application includes:

  - Admin Dashboard with KPIs and analytics
  - Order Management with multi-stage workflow (Order Hub)
  - Sales & Purchase Order management
  - Product Information System (PIS) with standalone layout
  - Goods Receiving (PO Requests, Issued POs, GRN List, Proofing, MRN/FG)
  - Manufacturing modules (BOM, Packaging, Raw Material management)
  - Treasury & Financial management
  - Role-based access control and User Management
  - Ticket/Enquiry management system
  - Task Management (BD Tasks, RND Tasks)
  - Vendor/Client management
  - Catalogue, Active Ingredients, and Packaging management
